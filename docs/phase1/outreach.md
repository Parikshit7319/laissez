## Rules I hold myself to

- One sandbox link per message. Never two.
- No deck attached to a first message. The one-pager goes out after a call or on request.
- No "quick call" or "pick your brain". Say 30 minutes and say what for.
- Never name the mutual contact who gave me a name unless they said I could.
- Reply to every reply within 24 hours, including the ones that say no.
